# Code of Conduct

MarketPulse welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Help make its issues, pull requests, and other project spaces
welcoming and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Accept corrections, take responsibility for mistakes, and allow others to learn.
- Respect privacy and use fictional financial records in public examples.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.
Repeated disruption or hostility after a request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in project-managed spaces and when someone is representing
the project publicly. Report concerns privately to the maintainer at
**brendan.hallas@hey.com** with links or relevant context. Share only the personal
information needed to explain the concern.

The maintainer will review reports as availability allows, handle them
discreetly, and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Possible actions
include removing content, requesting a change in behavior, restricting
participation, or banning a participant. Reports involving the maintainer may
also be raised through the platform's own reporting tools.
